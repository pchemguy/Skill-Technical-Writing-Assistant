# Composition exploration

> Status: reference skeleton. Scope and contract are established; detailed procedures, examples, and behavioral acceptance cases remain to be implemented.

## Scope and activation

Explore alternative framing, organizing principles, outlines, emphasis, and explanatory or argumentative sequences.

Use independently for a focused request or as a selected step in a composite workflow.

## Boundary

Propose alternatives; do not manufacture support or silently select a different authorial position.

## Inputs and missing information

Purpose, audience, source material, and any composition or argument findings.

Compare viable arrangements with trade-offs; recommend an approach tied to the writing purpose.

## Outputs

Alternative outlines or approaches, a recommendation, and substantive choices requiring author input.

## Completion checks

Alternatives preserve indispensable content and expose unsupported assumptions.

## Optional handoffs

Pass an authorized approach to structural-revision.md or draft-development.md.

Load only relevant references. A handoff does not automatically require author approval; continue within authorized scope and ask when a substantive choice remains unresolved.

## Implementation work remaining

Develop the step-by-step procedure, representative examples, failure handling, and observable acceptance cases for this contract. Do not describe this scaffold as behaviorally validated.
