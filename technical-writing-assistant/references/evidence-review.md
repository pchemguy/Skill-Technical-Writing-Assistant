# Evidence review

> Status: reference skeleton. Scope and contract are established; detailed procedures, examples, and behavioral acceptance cases remain to be implemented.

## Scope and activation

Identify claims requiring support, assess supplied evidence against the actual claims, and identify gaps and prospective evidence.

Use independently for a focused request or as a selected step in a composite workflow.

## Boundary

Own the relationship between claims and support; external retrieval belongs to source-research.md and requires the research mode.

## Inputs and missing information

Material, available evidence, relevant claims, and a selected evidence mode.

Resolve mode using manager.md#evidence-modes-and-selection when unclear. Separate inspected supplied material, unverified references, inference, and proposed support.

## Outputs

Claim-linked assessments, evidence gaps, prospective sources or evidence types, and verification status.

## Completion checks

Every assessment states its basis; prospective support and model recollection are not represented as independent verification.

## Optional handoffs

Use source-research.md only in external research mode. Pass proposed corrections to authorized revision workflows.

Load only relevant references. A handoff does not automatically require author approval; continue within authorized scope and ask when a substantive choice remains unresolved.

## Implementation work remaining

Develop the step-by-step procedure, representative examples, failure handling, and observable acceptance cases for this contract. Do not describe this scaffold as behaviorally validated.
