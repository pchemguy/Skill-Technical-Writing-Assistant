# Source research

> Status: reference skeleton. Scope and contract are established; detailed procedures, examples, and behavioral acceptance cases remain to be implemented.

## Scope and activation

Find and inspect external sources, fact-check relevant claims, verify prospective references, compare conflicting evidence, and record attribution.

Use independently for a focused request or as a selected step in a composite workflow.

## Boundary

External research mode only; do not silently change the author's conclusions or invent citations.

## Inputs and missing information

Claims or research questions, selected external-research mode, and suitable host-provided tools.

Inspect actual source content; record support, limitations, contradictions, and uncertainty. If tools are unavailable, disclose the limitation and offer the other mode; do not silently switch.

## Outputs

Source-backed findings with attribution, verification limits, and unresolved questions.

## Completion checks

Findings are tied to inspected material and actual tool use; source existence alone is not proof of support.

## Optional handoffs

Return findings to evidence-review.md; substantive correction choices remain explicit.

Load only relevant references. A handoff does not automatically require author approval; continue within authorized scope and ask when a substantive choice remains unresolved.

## Implementation work remaining

Develop the step-by-step procedure, representative examples, failure handling, and observable acceptance cases for this contract. Do not describe this scaffold as behaviorally validated.
