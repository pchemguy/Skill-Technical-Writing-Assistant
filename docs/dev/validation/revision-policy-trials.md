## Case 5a — Comprehensive editorial review, findings only

### Prompt and source

> Comprehensive editorial review, findings only. No source beyond this draft:
>
> # Reliability
> Install component A first. Time fell 40%, therefore cost fell 40%. dry-condition pilot: 12 devices, humidity untested. Widget and module refer to the same component.

### Action trace

1. Selected the manager's comprehensive editorial-review recipe, with composition, argument, evidence, language/style, and terminology coverage.
2. Did not choose research mode from silence. The exercise supplies no research authorization; completed internal editorial and supplied-content support assessment without retrieval.
3. Reviewed sentence, paragraph, and section composition; bounded document-level coverage to the excerpt.
4. Kept remedies as proposals and checked findings for consistency rather than generating a revision.

### Raw response

- **Major: the cost conclusion does not follow from the reported time change.** “Time fell 40%, therefore cost fell 40%” compares different quantities and supplies no cost model or cost measurements. Readers may mistake an unsupported inference for an observed result. Separate the reported time result from the cost claim and request support for the latter. Removing or narrowing the cost conclusion would be a substantive change.
- **Major: the “Reliability” heading does not describe the passage's full contents.** The paragraph mixes installation order, a performance/cost claim, pilot conditions, and a terminology definition. Reliability itself is not defined or directly measured here. Group setup instructions, results and limitations, and terminology separately; do not infer reliability from the pilot's existence.
- **Major: the pilot's relationship to the results is unclear.** The fragment supplies a dry-condition pilot, 12 devices, and no humidity testing, but does not say whether the 40% time result came from that pilot. Keep those limitations close to whichever result they qualify after confirming the connection. The sample and condition do not justify a broader environmental claim.
- **Moderate: the time result lacks a measurement definition and comparator.** Specify what “time” measures, the baseline, and the comparison conditions if known. The draft asserts the decrease; no source or measurement record independently supports it here. Missing support does not show that the assertion is false.
- **Moderate: “Install component A first” lacks procedure context.** The reader does not know what follows, how installation is performed, or the completion condition. Put it in the relevant setup procedure and add available context without inventing steps.
- **Moderate: terminology needs a defined naming policy.** “Widget” and “module” are declared synonymous. Choose a preferred prose term or explicitly introduce the alias where useful. The draft does not establish that either term refers to component A; avoid merging those concepts without confirmation.
- **Local: the pilot fragment interrupts continuous prose.** Capitalize and develop it into a sentence, or format it as a deliberate note/list item. “By 40%” is clearer than “40%” in the two reduction statements, but wording polish cannot repair the cost inference.

Coverage included sentence clarity and mechanics, paragraph focus and development, section heading and grouping, internal reasoning, supplied support and missing evidence, numerical scope and conditions, and terminology. Whole-document continuity, target audience conventions, implementation accuracy, measurement validity, and external corroboration are not assessable from this excerpt. No rewrite or retrieval was performed. Evidence-mode selection would be needed before any independent research.

### Observation and limits

The response covers relevant dimensions and supplies located, prioritized remedies without rewriting. It does not attach the time result to the pilot or collapse the synonymous terms into component A. It identifies the unresolved research choice if independent verification were to proceed; this exercise did not ask that choice because only work independent of retrieval was undertaken.
